## System Analysis & Lagrangian Descriptions
- Behaviour of a fluid (liquid or gas) can be predicted an studied by adopting one of the two:
	- system analysis and Lagrangian description of flow
	- control volume analysis and Eulerian description of flow
		- Eulerian is easier for fluids, as fluids deform and move 
### Lagrangian
![[Pasted image 20250109175210.png]]
- system analysis, identifianle assmeblage or grouping of fluid particls/elements or matter of fixed mass (the system). System may or may not be moving relative to ref frame
	- fluid can also move and change shape, but no mass enter or leaves the system i.e. mass stays constant
- Lagrangian description of flow, system analysis is adopted and indiv fluid elments/parcels of fixed mass are tracked through space and time along flow pathlines (flow properties and position must be tracked)
	-  can be complex for 3D gasdynamic apploications

### Control Volume analysis
![[Pasted image 20250109175619.png]]
- imaginary permeable closed surface (control surface) around control volume of intersest is used, and track the flow wihtin the contrl volum epassing through the control surface as fcn of time
	- system or sequence of multiples systems may flow through, but focus is not tracking individual flow element
- Eulerian description, control-volume analysis is adopted an dthe fluid motion + flow props. are then described as functions of/in terms of specific location in physical space and time. 
	- eliminates the need to identify and track individual particles
